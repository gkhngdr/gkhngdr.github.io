FootIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains what the FootIQ football quiz app (iOS, Android and web) collects, why, and how it is stored.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: if you create an account, your email address and the username you choose. Your password is stored encrypted; we cannot see it.
  • Game progress: coins, trophies, stars, level progress, daily reward streak, season ranking and shop items.
  • Guests: if you play without an account, your progress stays on your device only. Online matches use a random player ID and player name.
  • Online matches: the player name, look (avatar, scarf, title) and score shared with your opponent. Match rooms are deleted shortly after.
  • Voice answers (3-2-1 mode): the microphone is used only while you hold the mic button.
    • iOS and Android app: your speech is converted to text by your device's own speech recognition (Apple or Google); only the text reaches us.
    • Web version (iPhone/iPad browser): a recording of up to 8 seconds is sent through our server to our speech-recognition provider fal.ai to be transcribed. It is used only for this; we do not store it, and fal.ai keeps it only briefly to provide the service.
    The transcribed answer is used to check whether it is correct. Unrecognised answers may be stored anonymously to improve recognition.

2. How we use it

  • To let you sign in and carry your progress across devices
  • To run the season league and online matches
  • To send password-reset emails
  • To prevent cheating and abuse

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising. The app currently contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

3. Service providers

Account and game data are stored on Supabase. The web version is served by GitHub Pages. Voice answers in the web version are transcribed by fal.ai. These providers process data only to run the service.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app via Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and cloud data. If you can't open the app: account deletion page.
  • Guest data is removed from your device when you uninstall the app.

5. Children

FootIQ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR and Turkey's KVKK you have the right to access, correct and delete your data. Contact us at the address below.
